Judge the tests, not the code they test. Each should assert behaviour through the public interface, not implementation details, and fail if the change were reverted or broken: mutate the change, in your head or for real, and check that a test fails. Look for tests deleted, skipped or weakened to make the change pass, unrealistic fixtures, and concurrency or time tests that are not deterministic. A missing test for an acceptance criterion or a fixed defect is a finding.
